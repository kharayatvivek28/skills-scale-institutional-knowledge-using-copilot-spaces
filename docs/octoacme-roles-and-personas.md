# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## QA/Testing Lead

### Role Summary
QA/Testing Leads ensure product quality through systematic testing, acceptance criteria validation, and quality assurance across the delivery lifecycle.

### Responsibilities
- Design and execute test plans aligned with acceptance criteria
- Conduct unit, integration, and end-to-end testing
- Validate bug fixes and feature acceptance
- Coordinate security and compliance testing
- Document test results and quality metrics
- Identify and report defects with clear reproduction steps

### Goals
- Ensure product meets quality standards before release
- Reduce post-release defects and incidents
- Provide early feedback to prevent rework

### Typical Communication
- Test plans and results in weekly syncs
- Defect reports linked to backlog items
- Acceptance sign-off on feature completion

---

## Stakeholder/Sponsor

### Role Summary
Stakeholders and Sponsors provide business context, approve strategic decisions, and ensure alignment with organizational goals and priorities.

### Responsibilities
- Provide business context and success metrics for projects
- Approve project charter and release decisions
- Participate in escalation and risk resolution
- Receive and review status updates
- Advocate for project needs and secure resources

### Goals
- Ensure project delivers measurable business value
- Minimize business risk and maintain stakeholder confidence
- Align project outcomes with organizational strategy

### Typical Communication
- Monthly stakeholder updates and briefings
- Ad-hoc escalations and decision approvals
- Release announcements and impact assessments

---

## Product Lead

### Role Summary
Product Leads provide strategic oversight, mentor Product Managers, and ensure cross-project alignment with product vision and customer needs.

### Responsibilities
- Review and approve project charters and success metrics
- Mentor Product Managers and guide prioritization decisions
- Escalate risks and cross-project dependencies
- Ensure alignment with product vision and roadmap
- Facilitate resolution of conflicting priorities across projects

### Goals
- Ensure product strategy drives consistent delivery
- Enable PMs to make confident, data-informed decisions
- Reduce cross-project friction and rework

### Typical Communication
- Weekly PM sync participation and mentorship
- Project charter reviews and approvals
- Cross-project dependency coordination

---

## Security/Compliance Officer

### Role Summary
Security and Compliance Officers ensure that projects meet security, privacy, and regulatory requirements throughout the development lifecycle.

### Responsibilities
- Review and approve security requirements for projects
- Conduct or coordinate security reviews during execution
- Perform threat assessments and compliance checks
- Provide guidance on incident response and disclosure
- Track and report on security and compliance metrics

### Goals
- Prevent security incidents and compliance violations
- Enable secure, compliant product delivery
- Maintain customer and stakeholder trust

### Typical Communication
- Security requirements in planning documentation
- Code and design security reviews
- Incident escalation and response coordination

---

## Scrum Master / Agile Coach

### Role Summary
Scrum Masters and Agile Coaches facilitate team processes, remove impediments, and continuously improve team velocity and collaboration.

### Responsibilities
- Facilitate sprint planning, standups, and retrospectives
- Help teams understand and adopt Agile principles
- Remove blockers and facilitate dependency resolution
- Track team velocity and health metrics
- Coach continuous improvement based on retrospectives
- Protect team from scope creep and external distractions

### Goals
- Maximize team velocity and delivery consistency
- Create psychological safety and high team morale
- Embed continuous improvement into team culture

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint ceremonies
- Retrospective facilitation and action item tracking
- Velocity and impediment dashboards
